## 公司与商业模式

- 公司类型：Product manufacturer with multiple consumer durable and service robot lines: electric two-wheelers, scooters, balance scooters, all-terrain vehicles, mowing robots, e-bikes.
- 客户与价值：Simplifies and makes mobility and yard work convenient and fun. Sells smart personal electric vehicles and autonomous mowing robots globally through distributors, retail channels, and direct-to-consumer. Key customers: urban commuters, suburban families, fleet operators.
- 收入方程：Consolidated Revenue = Σ (Product_i volume × ASP_i) across regions and channels. Volume driven by end-user demand, dealer inventory, and new model launches. ASP affected by product mix, promotions, and geographic pricing.
- 利润方程：Gross Profit = Revenue - (Material + Conversion + Warranty). Operating Profit = Gross Profit - R&D - Selling - Administrative - FX gains/losses. Net Profit (parent) = Oper. Profit + Financial Income/Expense - Income Tax - Minority Interest.
- 现金方程：OCF = Net profit + D&A - ΔWorking Capital (focus on receivables, inventory, contract liabilities). FCF = OCF - Capex. Working capital sensitive to dealer stocking, seasonal build, and customer advances.
