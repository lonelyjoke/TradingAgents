### Company Operating Equations
- Revenue: Consolidated Revenue = Σ (Product_i volume × ASP_i) across regions and channels. Volume driven by end-user demand, dealer inventory, and new model launches. ASP affected by product mix, promotions, and geographic pricing.
- Profit: Gross Profit = Revenue - (Material + Conversion + Warranty). Operating Profit = Gross Profit - R&D - Selling - Administrative - FX gains/losses. Net Profit (parent) = Oper. Profit + Financial Income/Expense - Income Tax - Minority Interest.
- Cash flow: OCF = Net profit + D&A - ΔWorking Capital (focus on receivables, inventory, contract liabilities). FCF = OCF - Capex. Working capital sensitive to dealer stocking, seasonal build, and customer advances.
- Reinvestment: Moderate capex: tooling/molds for new models, assembly line expansion, headquarters construction. R&D spending for next-generation platforms. Cash also used for strategic equity investments and partnerships.
