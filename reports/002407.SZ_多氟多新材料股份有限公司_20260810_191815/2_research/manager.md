## Company Disaggregation

## Autonomous Three-Year Forecast Model

The accepted model is a consolidated, top-down/hybrid approach due to a lack of segment-level volume, price, and cost data. The primary driver is the recovery in the New Energy Materials (6F) segment. The three-year forecast hinges on the 6F price assumption. Revenue is built from an expected volume recovery (to 45,000 tons in 2026E) and a stabilized but not surging ASP. Gross margin recovers to 24% in 2026E (from an estimated 15% in 2025A), reflecting operating leverage on the volume recovery. ## Thesis-to-Financial Bridge

The core thesis bridge is: (6F Sales Volume (45k tons) × ASP (75k CNY/ton)) + (Other Revenue) = Consolidated Revenue (115bn CNY). Gross Profit = Revenue × Gross Margin (24.0%). Net Profit flows from Gross Profit, with the model bifurcation point being the sustainability of the margin. If the 6F ASP stabilizes >75k/ton (Bull Case), the 2027E EPS of 2.05 is possible. If the recent price weakness continues (Bear Case), 2026E EPS of 0.60 is more likely. The current share price bridges to the upper-end of the base case and the lower-end of the bull case, indicating a market expectation that is already optimistic.

## Moat Evidence Verdict

The company possesses a **proven cost advantage (partial moat)** in its core 6F business, derived from its integrated production chain (anhydrous hydrofluoric acid to 6F). This is a structural cost advantage versus non-integrated competitors. Evidence: higher-than-peer-average gross margin recovery in Q1 2026. This cost advantage is most impactful in a rising-price environment. In the Electronic Chemicals segment, the company has a **claimed technological moat** (UPSSS-grade HF qualification), which is a necessary entry ticket but does not guarantee a business moat without proven customer stickiness, pricing power, or revenue scale. The battery business has no observable moat.

The model therefore reflects a bimodal probability distribution, heavily contingent on August 18.

## Probability and Payoff

The probability-weighted fair value is 30.6 CNY/share versus a current price of ~36.5 CNY/share, implying a -16% expected return to fair value. The distribution is bimodal. The downside risk to the Bear case (9.9 CNY) is a catastrophic -73%, while the upside to the Bull case (51.3 CNY) is a strong +41%. The probability of the Bear case (20%) is non-trivial and the payoff magnitude is large, creating a negative skew in risk/reward. The positive expected value is therefore driven by a low-probability, high-payout event, which is not a suitable basis for a high-conviction investment without a very strong catalyst path. The decision is to wait for that catalyst.
