## 公司与商业模式

- 公司类型：Cyclical capital-goods manufacturer with global distribution; primary archetype is product/manufacturing (capacity → utilization → volume → ASP → input cost → margin), supplemented by project/order delivery for large equipment and solution bundles. Domestic replacement cycle plus overseas share gains drive the top line, while product mix, scale, and FX/input costs dictate margins.
- 客户与价值：Manufactures and sells excavators, cranes, concrete machinery, road machinery, and other construction equipment to infrastructure, mining, real estate, and energy customers in over 150 countries. Revenue is split between domestic (c.60% estimated) and international (c.40% estimated) markets. Domestic demand relies on equipment replacement, infrastructure policy, and real estate stabilization. Overseas demand is driven by resource capex, emerging-market urbanization, and global share migration from Western incumbents. No single customer exceeds 5% of revenue.
- 收入方程：Revenue = Σ (segment shipments × ASP) + parts & service revenue; shipments = capacity × utilization × yield. For large units a project/backlog element also exists.
- 利润方程：Gross profit = revenue - (raw materials + labour + manufacturing overhead + freight/warranty); Operating profit = gross profit - (R&D + SG&A + freight) ± other operating items; Parent profit = operating profit - net finance costs - tax - minority interest.
- 现金方程：OCF ≈ net profit + depreciation ± working capital change → high correlation with net profit historically (OCF/net profit ~1.4x); FCF = OCF - capex (maintenance + growth). Working capital is dominated by receivables and inventory, both closely linked to shipments and channel stocking.

## 七章路由

- **一、公司是谁、如何赚钱**：让普通投资者理解产品、客户、定价、收入确认、成本与现金回收。 （EV 12 条；KPE 0 条）
- **二、产业链位置与行业格局**：解释上下游、供需与价格形成，并把产业链变化传导到公司经营。 （EV 9 条；KPE 0 条）
- **三、业务拆解与核心利润池**：按核心、成长、波动和期权业务拆解收入、利润、现金与资本占用。 （EV 0 条；KPE 0 条）
- **四、竞争优势、护城河与主要短板**：用历史或真同行验证规模、品牌、渠道、技术和成本优势。 （EV 8 条；KPE 0 条）
- **五、增长逻辑、关键分歧与盈利预测**：围绕三至五个关键问题，把经营变量传导到三年盈利和现金流。 （EV 12 条；KPE 0 条）
- **六、市场预期差、风险与验证**：区分真实一致预期、单家卖方和反向隐含假设，并给出证伪日历。 （EV 4 条；KPE 6 条）
- **七、估值、评级与投资结论**：在完成公司、产业、模型和预期分析后，最后给出估值、评级与动作。 （EV 12 条；KPE 0 条）
