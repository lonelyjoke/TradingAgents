## 公司与商业模式

- 公司类型：Branded consumer-packaged-goods manufacturer and marketer, primarily functional energy drinks sold through multi-channel retail distribution
- 客户与价值：Provide affordable, readily available energy and electrolyte replenishment to blue-collar workers, drivers, students and active consumers via over 2 million points of sale; core product 东鹏特饮 competes on price-pack architecture and massive distribution coverage
- 收入方程：Revenue = Σ (active distribution points × average volume per point × product mix weight × realized ASP per unit) ≈ channel sell-through volume (cases/litres) × ASP × mix; growth comes from outlet expansion, same-outlet productivity and mix improvement
- 利润方程：Gross profit = Revenue - (direct raw materials + packaging + manufacturing conversion costs); Operating profit = Gross profit - selling & distribution expenses - administrative & R&D; Parent net profit = Operating profit - net finance expense + other income - income tax - minority interest
- 现金方程：OCF = Net profit + depreciation/amortisation – change in working capital (receivables, inventory, prepayments, payables, contract liabilities); FCF = OCF – capex (capacity, IT, maintenance); working capital seasonal, Q1 OCF/net profit low due to post-Chinese New Year channel restocking
