### Company Operating Equations
- Revenue: Revenue = Σ (active distribution points × average volume per point × product mix weight × realized ASP per unit) ≈ channel sell-through volume (cases/litres) × ASP × mix; growth comes from outlet expansion, same-outlet productivity and mix improvement
- Profit: Gross profit = Revenue - (direct raw materials + packaging + manufacturing conversion costs); Operating profit = Gross profit - selling & distribution expenses - administrative & R&D; Parent net profit = Operating profit - net finance expense + other income - income tax - minority interest
- Cash flow: OCF = Net profit + depreciation/amortisation – change in working capital (receivables, inventory, prepayments, payables, contract liabilities); FCF = OCF – capex (capacity, IT, maintenance); working capital seasonal, Q1 OCF/net profit low due to post-Chinese New Year channel restocking
- Reinvestment: Moderate capex intensity ~5-7% of revenue driven by nationwide production base network (14 planned, 12 operating) and expansion into new categories; construction-in-progress and land use rights significant; periodic募投项目 extensions suggest project timing risk
