## 公司与商业模式

- 公司类型：multi-product coal-based chemical manufacturer with flexible interlinked production
- 客户与价值：Produces ammonia, urea, methanol, acetic acid, DMF, adipic acid, caprolactam, nylon 6, and other chemicals using coal gasification, serving domestic agricultural, textile, polyurethane, and new-material industries; exports are growing but still secondary. Stable cooperation with >80% of customers are key industry leaders.
- 收入方程：Σ (segment_product_volume x realized_ASP_x_mix) + by-product and energy sales (electricity/steam)
- 利润方程：Revenue - coal/feedstock cost - energy and freight - conversion labor/overhead - depreciation - period expenses (selling, G&A, R&D, finance). Operating leverage is high due to fixed asset intensity.
- 现金方程：Operating profit + depreciation - change in working capital (receivables, inventory, payables) - capex (sustaining + growth) - tax - interest +/- financing
