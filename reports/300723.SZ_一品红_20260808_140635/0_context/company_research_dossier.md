## 公司与商业模式

- 公司类型：Pharmaceutical manufacturer with a separate innovative-drug pipeline (AR882) out-licensed to a global partner. The manufacturing business follows a volume × ASP × mix model driven by hospital procurement and national centralized drug procurement (集采). The pipeline operates as a pre-revenue R&D engine monetized through out-licensing upfront/milestone payments and potential future royalties.
- 客户与价值：Proprietary pharmaceutical products sold to hospitals and distributors in China via a marketing network and national centralized procurement. Innovative gout drug AR882 licensed to Sobi US for global development and commercialization in exchange for upfront, milestone, and royalty payments.
- 收入方程：Consolidated revenue = Pharmaceutical Manufacturing revenue (volume × ASP × procurement share) + Licensing revenue (milestone/upfront recognition). Note: Upfront payments may flow through non-operating income rather than revenue.
- 利润方程：Consolidated net profit = Manufacturing gross profit - Selling/R&D/G&A - Finance cost + Non-operating gains (Sobi upfront, asset disposal) - Tax. Recurring operating profit (扣非后净利润) was negative in Q1 2026 despite headline net profit +950.35%.
- 现金方程：Operating cash flow currently weak relative to accounting profit (OCF/net profit 0.0315 in Q1 2026). Cash inflows depend on Sobi upfront receipt, collection of manufacturing receivables, and eventual milestone payments. Outflows are driven by R&D expense, capex, and working capital.

## 七章路由

- **一、公司是谁、如何赚钱**：让普通投资者理解产品、客户、定价、收入确认、成本与现金回收。 （EV 12 条；KPE 0 条）
- **二、产业链位置与行业格局**：解释上下游、供需与价格形成，并把产业链变化传导到公司经营。 （EV 10 条；KPE 0 条）
- **三、业务拆解与核心利润池**：按核心、成长、波动和期权业务拆解收入、利润、现金与资本占用。 （EV 0 条；KPE 0 条）
- **四、竞争优势、护城河与主要短板**：用历史或真同行验证规模、品牌、渠道、技术和成本优势。 （EV 7 条；KPE 0 条）
- **五、增长逻辑、关键分歧与盈利预测**：围绕三至五个关键问题，把经营变量传导到三年盈利和现金流。 （EV 12 条；KPE 0 条）
- **六、市场预期差、风险与验证**：区分真实一致预期、单家卖方和反向隐含假设，并给出证伪日历。 （EV 1 条；KPE 1 条）
- **七、估值、评级与投资结论**：在完成公司、产业、模型和预期分析后，最后给出估值、评级与动作。 （EV 12 条；KPE 0 条）
