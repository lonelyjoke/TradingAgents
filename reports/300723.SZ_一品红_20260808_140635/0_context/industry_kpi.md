## General Operating-Model Router
- These are deterministic candidates, not final classifications. Assign a primary and optional secondary profile to each material business unit; a diversified company may use multiple profiles.
### Candidate: bank_spread_credit (bank / spread and credit; score=21)
- Revenue equation: earning assets x NIM + fee/other income
- Profit equation: net revenue - operating cost - credit cost/provisions - tax
- Cash equation: capital generation - RWA growth - dividend; cash-flow statement is not a manufacturing FCF proxy
- Mandatory KPIs: earning assets and NIM; deposit cost/mix; NPL and provision coverage; credit cost; CET1/RWA and payout
- Competition dimensions: funding franchise; customer/branch ecosystem; loan pricing; risk selection; digital efficiency; regulatory capital
- Valuation methods: PB-ROE; P/E; dividend discount/residual income
- Falsification gates: asset quality deteriorates; capital constrains growth/payout; funding cost prevents NIM stabilization
### Candidate: project_order_delivery (project / order / equipment delivery; score=20)
- Revenue equation: opening backlog + new orders - delivered/recognized orders = ending backlog
- Profit equation: recognized project revenue - BOM/labor/subcontract/warranty - R&D and selling overhead
- Cash equation: cash advances + collections - procurement/WIP - delivery/acceptance working capital - capex
- Mandatory KPIs: new orders and backlog; book-to-bill; delivery/acceptance cadence; project gross margin; receivables/contract assets and collections
- Competition dimensions: technical qualification; installed base/service; price and delivery; customer capex; localization; new entrants
- Valuation methods: order-backed DCF; EV/EBITDA; SOTP by equipment/project category
- Falsification gates: orders fail to convert; acceptance or collection delays; backlog margin deteriorates
### Candidate: pharma_biotech (pharma / biotech; score=20)
- Revenue equation: commercial products: patients x penetration x net price; pipeline: probability-adjusted eligible population/value
- Profit equation: product gross profit - selling/medical affairs - R&D by program - milestone/royalty economics
- Cash equation: commercial cash contribution - R&D - milestone obligations - capex +/- working capital and financing
- Mandatory KPIs: patient/volume and net price; market share and competing labels; clinical/regulatory milestones; R&D spend by stage; cash runway and dilution
- Competition dimensions: clinical differentiation; label and reimbursement; physician/patient switching; competing pipeline timing; manufacturing/IP; BD alternatives
- Valuation methods: risk-adjusted NPV; SOTP; DCF for commercial assets
- Falsification gates: clinical/regulatory failure; commercial uptake misses; cash runway forces dilutive financing
