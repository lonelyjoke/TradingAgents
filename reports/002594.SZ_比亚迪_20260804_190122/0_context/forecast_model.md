### Company Operating Equations
- Revenue: Consolidated revenue = ∑ (segment volume × realized ASP) + incidental revenue. Volume driven by NEV unit sales, battery shipments, and mobile component deliveries. ASP influenced by product mix, export/domestic split, and competitive dynamics.
- Profit: Gross profit = revenue - raw materials (battery metals, semiconductors, steel, etc.) - manufacturing costs (labor, depreciation, energy). Operating profit = gross profit - R&D - selling/admin expenses. Net profit = operating profit ± finance costs ± investment income ± asset impairments - tax - minority interests.
- Cash flow: OCF = net profit + non-cash charges (D&A, impairments) ± working capital (inventory, receivables, payables). FCF = OCF - capex (factory, R&D capitalization, overseas expansion). Heavy investment cycle with significant capex for capacity expansion and technology development.
- Reinvestment: Capital-intensive with ~10% revenue spent on capex and >8% on R&D. Reinvestment needed for capacity growth, new model launches, battery technology, and overseas market entry. High fixed asset base and ongoing working capital requirements constrain immediate FCF generation.
