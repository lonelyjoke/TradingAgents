## General Operating-Model Router
- These are deterministic candidates, not final classifications. Assign a primary and optional secondary profile to each material business unit; a diversified company may use multiple profiles.
### Candidate: project_order_delivery (project / order / equipment delivery; score=24)
- Revenue equation: opening backlog + new orders - delivered/recognized orders = ending backlog
- Profit equation: recognized project revenue - BOM/labor/subcontract/warranty - R&D and selling overhead
- Cash equation: cash advances + collections - procurement/WIP - delivery/acceptance working capital - capex
- Mandatory KPIs: new orders and backlog; book-to-bill; delivery/acceptance cadence; project gross margin; receivables/contract assets and collections
- Competition dimensions: technical qualification; installed base/service; price and delivery; customer capex; localization; new entrants
- Valuation methods: order-backed DCF; EV/EBITDA; SOTP by equipment/project category
- Falsification gates: orders fail to convert; acceptance or collection delays; backlog margin deteriorates
### Candidate: standard_manufacturing (standardized manufacturing; score=21)
- Revenue equation: effective capacity x utilization x yield x saleable volume x realized ASP/mix
- Profit equation: volume x (realized ASP - material - conversion - freight - warranty) - period expense
- Cash equation: EBITDA - working-capital build - maintenance/growth capex - tax/interest
- Mandatory KPIs: capacity/utilization/yield; shipment and ASP/mix; unit material/conversion cost; inventory and receivables; incremental ROIC
- Competition dimensions: cost curve; quality/yield; customer qualification; scale; capacity response; substitution
- Valuation methods: EV/EBITDA; DCF/FCF; PE/PB-ROE cross-check
- Falsification gates: utilization or yield weakens; price erosion outruns cost reduction; expansion earns below cost of capital
### Candidate: resource_commodity (resource / commodity cycle; score=18)
- Revenue equation: equity output x realized product price plus by-product credits
- Profit equation: output x (realized price - cash cost/AISC) - sustaining overhead
- Cash equation: cash margin - sustaining/growth capex - tax/royalty/minority - working capital
- Mandatory KPIs: reserves/grade/mine life; equity output; realized price/basis; cash cost/AISC; sustaining capex and leverage
- Competition dimensions: cost-curve position; resource quality; jurisdiction/license; supply discipline; substitution; capital-cycle response
- Valuation methods: NAV/SOTP; normalized EV/EBITDA; cycle-trough PB/PE
- Falsification gates: cost curve moves above peers; project ramp or reserve conversion fails; balance sheet cannot survive trough

## Analyst Instructions
- Do not let a generic sector label replace the KPI map. State which KPI layers are verified, partial, or missing.
- If the report uses cycle language, connect it to at least demand, price/spread, inventory/backlog, and capacity/utilization evidence.
- For semiconductor companies, do not use consumer-style PE shortcuts. Split core value, cycle value, and verified product/technology optionality; triangulate PE with EV/EBITDA, DCF/FCF, ROIC/PB or SOTP as appropriate.
- If a thesis-critical KPI is missing, move it to the retrieval/verification calendar as neutral non-evidence instead of converting it into a hard trigger or rating adjustment.
