## 公司与商业模式

- 公司类型：Integrated manufacturer of new energy vehicles (NEV), batteries, mobile components and assemblies. The company sells finished automobiles, battery packs, and electronic components to consumers, fleet operators, and OEM customers through dealerships, direct sales, and B2B channels.
- 客户与价值：Provides affordable, technology-driven NEVs with vertical integration from battery to vehicle. Also supplies key components (batteries, electronic modules) to external OEMs and aftermarket customers. Primary customer groups: individual car buyers, corporate fleets, public transport operators, consumer electronics brands (Apple, Xiaomi), and overseas markets.
- 收入方程：Consolidated revenue = ∑ (segment volume × realized ASP) + incidental revenue. Volume driven by NEV unit sales, battery shipments, and mobile component deliveries. ASP influenced by product mix, export/domestic split, and competitive dynamics.
- 利润方程：Gross profit = revenue - raw materials (battery metals, semiconductors, steel, etc.) - manufacturing costs (labor, depreciation, energy). Operating profit = gross profit - R&D - selling/admin expenses. Net profit = operating profit ± finance costs ± investment income ± asset impairments - tax - minority interests.
- 现金方程：OCF = net profit + non-cash charges (D&A, impairments) ± working capital (inventory, receivables, payables). FCF = OCF - capex (factory, R&D capitalization, overseas expansion). Heavy investment cycle with significant capex for capacity expansion and technology development.
