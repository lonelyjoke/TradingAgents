# Research Manager Accepted Underwriting Model

**Rating**: Hold

**Research Readiness**: partial

**Core Bet**: The investment case for 002594.SZ pivots on its ability to translate explosive export volume growth into verifiably profitable earnings, offsetting a severe domestic price war. The core underwriting bet is whether BYD can achieve a sustainable 5-8% net margin on its overseas NEV business by 2027, which is currently an unsubstantiated channel-check narrative rather than a filing-backed fact. The market prices stagnation (implied TTM profit of ~275 bn CNY vs. 326 bn in FY2025). Our base case estimates this is achievable, providing a positive expected value, but the probability-weighted upside (+23%) is not yet sufficiently asymmetric against the verified downside risk of continued margin erosion.

### 本报告要回答的关键问题

1. Can BYD translate its verified export volume surge (over 70% YoY growth) into a profitable revenue stream with a net margin significantly above its domestic operations?
2. Will the tailwind from lithium carbonate prices at the 7.1st percentile finally flow through to the consolidated gross margin, or is this structurally offset by the domestic NEV price war?
3. Is the heavy capex cycle peaking in 2026, creating a credible path to free cash flow breakeven by 2027-2028, or will continued investment drag on returns?
4. Does the unverified new business narrative around AI infrastructure (AIDC) and robotics represent a real, quantifiable call option, or a sentiment-driven distraction from a deteriorating core auto business?
5. Is BYD the best-positioned auto OEM in its peer group to navigate the current industry downcycle, considering its scale, cost structure, and balance sheet versus peers with lower valuations or higher export elasticity?

### 核心问题裁决

**Q1：UQ02: Can BYD translate its verified export volume surge into a profitable revenue stream?**
- **为何关键：** Export is the single largest growth driver. If export margins are thin, the bull thesis collapses and the stock de-rates to a value-trap multiple. If they are high, a significant earnings upgrade cycle begins.
- **当前结论：** Export profitability is a high-impact but unverified assumption. Volume evidence is strong, but there is zero filing-backed margin data. The bull uses an unverified Citigroup conditional forecast, while the bear correctly identifies that a blended gross margin decline amid low lithium prices is a strong counter-signal implying poor incremental profitability.
- **采用证据：** KPE03: European real sales +138% YoY (unverified channel check)；KPE10: July exports +124% YoY (unverified channel check)；EV012: Q1 2026 consolidated gross margin fell 1.26pp YoY to 18.81% despite low lithium costs.；EV015: Finance expense ratio rose 2.52pp YoY, likely from overseas expansion funding.
- **最强反证：** If export net margins were truly incrementally positive, the rapid mix shift towards exports (44% of volume) should have stabilized, not eroded, the consolidated Q1 2026 gross margin.
- **模型/估值影响：** The bull's base case requires Q2-Q4 profit to be over 2.5x Q1's run-rate. The export margin assumption is the most sensitive variable determining if this is possible. We maintain a watchful stance and verify before upgrading.
- **置信度与下次验证：** low；Q2 2026 (H1 2026) Earnings Release: Geographic segment revenue, gross margin, or management commentary on per-unit export profitability. A second-best proxy is whether consolidated gross margin rebounds sequentially from Q1's 18.81%.

**Q2：UQ03: Will the lithium carbonate cost tailwind flow through to margins?**
- **为何关键：** This is the primary cost-side catalyst. A failure to capture this tailwind means the price war is so severe that the core cost-leadership moat has been neutralized, fundamentally changing the earnings power.
- **当前结论：** The cost tailwind has been completely absorbed by the domestic price war and product mix deterioration. The bear's argument that this proves the price war is an insurmountable structural headwind is too strong, but the bull's claim of a 'future reserve of profit' remains entirely unproven after Q1 2026.
- **采用证据：** EV034: Lithium carbonate at 139,380 CNY/ton, 7.1st percentile historically.；EV012: Q1 2026 gross margin fell Y/Y despite the lithium price drop.；EV038: Revenue growth -81.31% QoQ in Q1 2026, indicating severe pricing pressure.
- **最强反证：** The Q1 2026 margin result directly contradicts the thesis that lower lithium costs are a near-term margin catalyst.
- **模型/估值影响：** We accept the bear's lower conviction for a margin recovery, keeping our 2026E gross margin assumption conservative at 19.5%. No upward revision is justified yet.
- **置信度与下次验证：** medium；Q2 2026 consolidated gross margin. Any level above 20% would be a positive signal.

**Q3：UQ04: When will FCF turn sustainably positive?**
- **为何关键：** A negative FCF profile in a competitive, capital-intensive industry exposes the company to dilution or debt risk if the cycle worsens. A turning point is the key signal for quality maturation.
- **当前结论：** We accept the bull's thesis that capex is likely peaking, making a 2027-2028 FCF inflection plausible. However, the bear's focus on Q1 OCF being down 49% is a severe warning that working capital is a massive cash drain, not just capex.
- **采用证据：** EV030: Planned 19.7 bn CNY for overseas expansion, suggesting a capex peak.；EV041: Q1 2026 OCF down 49.24% YoY.；EV044: Inventory/revenue ratio up 4pp YoY, confirming working capital pressure identified by the bear.
- **最强反证：** Even if capex peaks, the sharp deterioration in operating cash flow from ballooning inventory suggests the FCF problem is deeper than a simple investment cycle.
- **模型/估值影响：** We maintain our forecast of deeply negative FCF in 2026. The path to positive FCF requires inventory management to improve, a fact neither side has a strong handle on.
- **置信度与下次验证：** medium；H1 2026 cash flow statement to see if the Q1 OCF drop was seasonal or the start of a structural trend.

**Q4：UQ05: Is the AI/robotics narrative a real call option?**
- **为何关键：** This is the primary source of potential valuation re-rating outside of the core auto business. In the A-share market, such themes can impact sentiment.
- **当前结论：** The bear is correct that this is an unverified, speculative narrative. The 800G optical module business is real per channel checks, but it is a highly competitive, low-margin segment for a newcomer against established players like Innolight. The bull cannot quantify its financial impact.
- **采用证据：** KPE01: BYD Electronic progressing on AI liquid cooling and power.；KPE02: Competition in optical modules is intense; pricing pressure is high.；KPE05: BYD robot to be potentially unveiled in August.；EV043: Non-recurring impairment in Q1 2026 indicates potential risks in new ventures.
- **最强反证：** Established optical module leaders have a massive R&D and capacity lead. BYD Electronics entering this space is a 'show-me' story with a low probability of disrupting incumbents at high margins.
- **模型/估值影响：** We retain a small option value (CNY 5.0/share) in the bull scenario only. It is removed from our base case core valuation until filing-backed revenue evidence emerges.
- **置信度与下次验证：** low；BYD Electronic (0285.HK) H1 2026 interim report for standalone financials and segment breakdown.

## Company Disaggregation

The company is an integrated manufacturer with two primary economic engines:
1. **New Energy Vehicles & Battery (Core Mature Value, ~81% of FY2025 Revenue):** A volume x price x cost model. Within this, the critical split is geography (Domestic vs. Overseas), as the domestic market is a saturated, price-war-ridden profit pool with eroding margins, while the overseas market is a high-growth segment with a still-unquantified profitability premium. The economic engine has shifted from domestic volume scale to export profitability, a variable not disclosed in current filings. This unit is a cash-absorbing cycle business with heavy capex and working capital drag.
2. **Mobile Components & Assembly (Secondary Revenue Driver, ~15-19% of Revenue):** A business that trades technology and scale for low, stable margins, serving major OEMs like Apple. It provides diversification but little earnings upside. The narrative around an AI/robotics second curve is an unproven, high-risk call option attached to this unit. The parent-level economic reality is a fight for survival in a deflationary domestic market, masked by the optics of booming export unit sales.

## Thesis-to-Financial Bridge

1. **Thesis: Export-Driven Margin Recovery**

   - Base Case: Domestic Net Margin falls to 0%, Export Net Margin rises to 6%. This yields 45 bn CNY in 2027E group net profit.
   - Counter-Thesis (Bear): Export Net Margin is 0-2%. Group earnings are capped at 25 bn CNY.

   - Formula: Probability of Success * (2030 Revenue * Peak Net Margin / Terminal Value Multiple).
   - Base Case: 15% probability of an outcome worth 60 bn CNY in 2030, heavily discounted.

## Moat Evidence Verdict

BYD's competitve advantages are under severe stress in the current cycle:
- **Cost Leadership (Proven but Under Siege):** The company's massive scale demonstrably delivers industry-leading cost per vehicle. However, the market evidence (Q1 margins) shows that this moat provides survivability, not pricing power. This is a war-time moat, not a peace-time moat.
- **Vertical Integration (Partial):** The integration from battery to vehicle is a real asset, but its benefit is not visible in the current financials, as low lithium costs are not translating to margin expansion. This suggests the internalized supply chain is not providing a sufficient buffer against external pricing shocks.
- **Technology/Brand (Unproven):** The technology is a necessary condition to compete, but the inability to sustain a non-price brand appeal (a frequent investor concern per the interaction log) means the moat is primarily cost-based, which is weaker than a durable brand moat. The statement by the bull that the `moat relies on cost leadership rather than pricing power, which is weaker but hard to erode quickly` is accepted by this committee.

## Debate Verdict

The bear won the tactical, evidence-based battle by correctly focusing on the hard, factual deterioration in Q1 2026 margins and cash flow — data points the bull could not explain away. The bull relied on unverified channel checks and forward-looking narratives (export margins, AI optionality) that lack filing-backed evidence. However, the bear's strategic inference — that the negative Q1 data is a permanent structural shift and the stock is a value trap — is weaker. We accept the bear's evidence as a reason to resist the bull's call to immediate action, but we do not accept the bear's thesis as a reason to sell. The investment case is one of 'unresolved asymmetry.'
