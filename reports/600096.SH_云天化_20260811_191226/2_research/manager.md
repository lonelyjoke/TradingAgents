# Research Manager Accepted Underwriting Model

**Rating**: Hold

**Research Readiness**: partial

**Core Bet**: Phosphate fertilizer margin recovery during autumn demand window (Aug-Oct 2026). The stock is a bet on whether verified supply-side discipline and seasonal demand can lift realized DAP/MAP prices and widen margins beyond the steady-state already priced at ~10.3x TTM PE. Upside requires autumn ASP improvement >5%; downside catalyst is sulfur cost rebound or demand disappointment compressing Q3 margins.

### 本报告要回答的关键问题

1. What is the realized phosphate fertilizer price, volume, and cash production cost for the next three seasons?

### 核心问题裁决

**Q1：What is the realized phosphate fertilizer price, volume, and cash production cost for the next three seasons?**
- **为何关键：** Phosphate fertilizers are the dominant profit driver; a 5% price or cost change can swing EPS by 15-20%.
- **当前结论：** Channel intelligence (KPE03, KPE04, KPE06) points consistently to tight phosphate rock supply near 1000 CNY/t, seasonal autumn demand recovery, and sulfur cost relief that could widen spreads. However, company-specific realized ASP, exact volumes, and unit cash costs are missing, limiting conviction to scenario-stage rather than model-hardened.
- **采用证据：** KPE03 (2026-07-27): phosphate rock supply-demand balance supporting 1000 CNY/t floor；KPE06 (2026-07-22): autumn fertilizer demand verification + raw material cost easing driving spread repair；KPE02 (2026-08-10): cautious downstream buying, restocking not yet visible；EV012 (Q1 2026): gross margin 19.56%, YoY improvement but still at mid-cycle level
- **最强反证：** KPE02 suggests downstream buyers remain cautious and restocking has not started; sulfur price volatility creates two-sided risk; Q1 gross margin improvement (19.56%) is modest and could reverse if costs rise before prices.
- **模型/估值影响：** Base scenario margin at 19.6% in 2026E remains unchanged. Bull case (25% probability) lifts EPS from 2.85 to 3.29 CNY/share. Bear case (15% probability) drops EPS to 2.19 CNY/share. Probability-weighted fair value ~29.98 CNY/share.
- **置信度与下次验证：** medium；H1 2026 interim report segment disclosure (late Aug 2026) for realized phosphate fertilizer ASP and volumes; autumn fertilizer price index movement through Sep-Oct 2026.

**Q2：Can phosphate rock output and grade be maintained without a step-up in sustaining capex?**
- **为何关键：** Self-sufficiency in phosphate rock is the core moat; rising extraction costs or falling grade would structurally erode integrated margins and ROIC.
- **当前结论：** Policy evidence (EV035) supports resource scarcity and new mine restrictions. Domestic grade decline (EV023) creates a long-term headwind, but the pace and company-specific impact remain unquantified. The bull thesis of a sustainable cost advantage is directionally supported but unproven without mine-level data.
- **采用证据：** EV035: policy restricting new phosphate mine approvals, reinforcing resource scarcity；EV023: declining average domestic phosphate rock grade over years, implying rising extraction costs
- **最强反证：** EV023 directly challenges the perpetual-cost-advantage assumption—if grade decline accelerates, sustaining capex and unit costs could rise faster than expected, compressing margins even in favorable price environments.
- **模型/估值影响：** No change to 2026E-2028E margins or capex assumptions. Phosphate rock cost advantage is maintained as a qualitative moat but is downgraded from proven to partial until mine cost data is disclosed.
- **置信度与下次验证：** low；Annual report mining segment operating data, including reserves, grade, output, and cash cost per tonne.

**Q3：How will capital allocation shift between high dividend payout and growth capex?**
- **为何关键：** The stock's defensive appeal rests on high dividend yield (~4.7% on 2025 distribution). A shift toward aggressive capex would reduce FCF available for dividends and alter the investment case from income to growth.
- **当前结论：** Current evidence supports dividend sustainability in the near term (OCF/NP ratio 1.69x in Q1 2026; historical payout commitment). However, 2025 investing cash outflow increased by 480 mn CNY due to construction-in-progress, creating a watch item for potential future strain.
- **采用证据：** EV016: Q1 2026 OCF/net profit ratio 1.69, strong cash conversion；EV015: 2025 investing cash outflow +480 mn CNY, construction-in-progress expansion；Management capital-allocation context: 2025 annual dividend 1.2 CNY/10 shares + interim 0.2 CNY/10 shares
- **最强反证：** The 2025 investing cash outflow increase signals a possible capex cycle. If sustained or accelerated, FCF and dividend coverage could be squeezed even before net profit declines.
- **模型/估值影响：** FCF assumption of ~6.4 bn CNY in base case maintained. Dividend yield provides valuation floor support in bear scenario (implied ~4.7% yield at 29.83 CNY), reinforcing the bear-case floor near 20 CNY/share (9x P/E).
- **置信度与下次验证：** medium；Management conference call or H1 2026 report for detailed capex budget guidance and updated dividend policy statement.

## Company Disaggregation

Yuntianhua operates an integrated phosphate mining-to-fertilizer business model with five economic units. **Phosphate Fertilizers (MAP/DAP/TSP)** is the dominant profit pool (analytically >50% of gross profit), driven by captive phosphate rock, realized fertilizer ASP, and sulfur cost. This is the core resource-cyclical engine with the highest valuation sensitivity. **Nitrogen Fertilizers (Urea, Ammonia)** provides stable cash flow, with profitability driven by the urea-coal spread. **Compound Fertilizers** is a value-added product line with customer stickiness but lower margin contribution. **Polyoxymethylene (POM)** is the second growth curve: an engineering plastics business with higher margins and import-substitution potential, but currently too small to independently anchor valuation; it remains a scenario option. **Trading and Logistics** is low-margin, pass-through revenue that inflates the top line but contributes negligible profit. All segment revenue weights, margins, and growth rates are missing from the current evidence set; the above profit-pool ranking is analytical only. Segment economics must be verified from the H1 2026 interim report "分产品" (by-product) disclosure. The competitive position is strongest in phosphate fertilizers (captive rock, policy-protected supply, cost-curve advantage) and most uncertain in POM (potential oversupply, no verified ASP/capacity/utilization data).
