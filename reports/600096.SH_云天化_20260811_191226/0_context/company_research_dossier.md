## 公司与商业模式

- 公司类型：Integrated phosphate resource and chemical manufacturer with fertilizer, POM and trading
- 客户与价值：Phosphate rock self-sufficiency, large-scale phosphate/nitrogen fertilizer production, and engineering plastic (POM) sales to domestic and overseas agricultural and industrial customers
- 收入方程：Sum over products: volume_sold * realized_ASP + trading revenue
- 利润方程：Gross profit (volume*(realized price - unit cost)) - selling/administrative/R&D expenses - finance costs + other income - income tax - minority interest
- 现金方程：Net operating profit + depreciation - change in working capital - capex - dividends
