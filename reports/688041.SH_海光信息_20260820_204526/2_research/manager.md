# Research Manager Accepted Underwriting Model

**Rating**: Underweight

**Research Readiness**: partial

**Core Bet**: The core bet is not whether China AI capex is strong, but whether 688041.SH can convert that demand into verified H2 2026-2028 revenue, stable margin and positive cash conversion against a 182x TTM PE. The accepted base case uses FY2026E revenue of CNY 18,198.17 mn and parent net profit of CNY 3,099.93 mn; the current price already embeds faster DCU4/supernode conversion and margin recovery than the verified evidence supports.

### 本报告要回答的关键问题

1. UQ01: Can DCU4 and system-level delivery convert into confirmed revenue and orders in H2 2026-2027, rather than industry narrative only?
2. UQ02: Is the Q1 2026 gross margin decline of 5.59pp product structure, cost, or price competition; where will 2026-2028 gross/net margin settle?
3. UQ03: Is 2026H1 OCF -427.5 mn strategic inventory build or demand/receivables deterioration; when does cash conversion recover?
4. UQ04: Can actual capacity, utilization, advanced-process/advanced-packaging supply support explosive shipments without yield or cost loss?
5. UQ05: At PE TTM 182x, what 3-5 year profit and ROIC path is implied, and is the disclosed annual profit/EPS proxy reliable?

### 核心问题裁决

**Q1：Can DCU4 and system-level delivery convert into confirmed revenue and orders in H2 2026-2027, rather than industry narrative only?**
- **为何关键：** It directly drives FY2026-2028 revenue growth and decides whether the 182x PE can be digested by volume, profit and order evidence rather than by multiple expansion.
- **当前结论：** Not proven. H1 2026 delivery is real, but the H2 bridge depends on new DCU4/order disclosure that the company has not published. Bull evidence from KPE05/KPE08 is channel and sell-side material, not company official disclosure. Contract liabilities fell 99.04% to CNY 19.36 mn, which confirms prior delivery but weakens near-term backlog visibility.
- **采用证据：** KPE05: industry note says 2026 supernode penetration 20% and 2027 double; DCU4 H2 launch expected；KPE08: sell-side push that system-level delivery is the key item requiring verification for Hygon；KPE02: channel check target mention only; verification item；Filing: 2026H1 contract liabilities CNY 19,358,226.08 vs prior CNY 2,018,511,039.57, -99.04% due completed product delivery
- **最强反证：** 2026H1 implied revenue of about CNY 9,099 mn plus official H1 revenue growth guidance of +55.56% to +70.20% suggests real end demand; the contract-liability draw-down partly reflects delivery rather than order loss.
- **模型/估值影响：** No change to base 2026E_revenue of CNY 18,198.17 mn; bull scenario 2026E_revenue of CNY 22,000 mn remains conditional upside. Bull probability kept at 30% instead of being raised to 40%.
- **置信度与下次验证：** low；2026 half-year full report and Q3 2026 product/segment revenue and order backlog disclosures; company guidance on DCU4 revenue recognition.

**Q2：Is the Q1 2026 gross margin decline of 5.59pp product structure, cost, or price competition; where will 2026-2028 gross/net margin settle?**
- **为何关键：** Margin is the bridge from revenue to profit and EPS. If mainstream price competition is emerging, high revenue growth will not produce high profit growth and the 182x PE loses support.
- **当前结论：** Margin pressure is verified but cause is unresolved. Q1 2026 gross margin is still high at 55.604%, but fell 5.59pp; net margin of 17.0343% fell 4.04pp. Selling expense rose 260.69% and R&D rose 55.05%, indicating reinvestment and competitive intensity. Official H1 ex-share-based payment profit growth supports operating quality, but does not prove gross margin stabilization.
- **采用证据：** EV012: Q1 2026 gross margin 55.604%, YoY -5.59pp；EV014: Q1 2026 net margin 17.0343%, YoY -4.04pp；Debate official H1 2026 profit forecast: parent net profit +41.50%-52.32%; ex share-based payment +74.27%-84.71%；Filing: selling expense +260.69%; R&D +55.05%
- **最强反证：** Q1 gross margin remains above 55% absolute; product mix shift toward inference could improve structure, and official ex-share-based payment profit growth is strong.
- **模型/估值影响：** Base 2026E net margin remains 17.0343%; bull 18.5% remains scenario-only; bear 13.5% probability reduced from 25% to 20% because a sharp H2 demand collapse is less supported.
- **置信度与下次验证：** medium；2026 interim report product cost/segment margin and Q3 2026 gross margin q/q.

**Q3：Is 2026H1 OCF -427.5 mn strategic inventory build or demand/receivables deterioration; when does cash conversion recover?**
- **为何关键：** FCF and growth quality determine the warranted multiple. High inventory and negative OCF raise impairment and valuation risk.
- **当前结论：** Delivery explains part of the working-capital drag because contract liabilities were consumed to complete product delivery. However, Q1 OCF/net profit of 0.098 and H1 OCF of -427.54 mn are hard cash-quality warnings. Without the cash-flow supplement, no full-year FCF forecast should be accepted. The observed pattern is not yet proven as demand deterioration, so it does not independently force the bear case.
- **采用证据：** EV036: 2026H1 operating cash flow -427,541,001.36；EV039: related OCF/working-capital evidence；EV042: 2026H1 inventory book value CNY 7,518.22 mn; 20.86% of total assets；EV016: cash-flow/covenant supporting evidence；Filing: contract liabilities -99.04% due completed product delivery
- **最强反证：** 2025H1 OCF was +2,176,641,209.26; the swing is material and could reflect receivables collection or inventory overbuild, not just delivery timing.
- **模型/估值影响：** No 2026E OCF, capex or FCF value accepted. The cash-quality risk caps the base multiple at 140x.
- **置信度与下次验证：** low；2026 interim cash-flow supplement, inventory product breakdown, receivable turnover days.

**Q4：Can actual capacity, utilization, advanced-process/advanced-packaging supply support explosive shipments without yield or cost loss?**
- **为何关键：** Volume is the core revenue driver; if capacity or yield is limited, revenue growth below market expectations will challenge the valuation.
- **当前结论：** Unresolved. Macro industry evidence points to improving domestic AI semiconductor supply, but there is no company-disclosed output, utilization, yield, or supplier capacity data. This makes the CNY 22,000 mn bull revenue a top-down, low-confidence assumption.
- **采用证据：** KPE01: Morgan Stanley industry view on cloud AI semiconductor growth and advanced packaging capacity；KPE05: domestic AI chip iteration and accelerated shipment industry note；KPE06: capacity/production-related evidence cited in UQ04 but not directly substantiated in the provided stream
- **最强反证：** No company data on production/sales volume, utilization, foundry/package suppliers, yield, or unit cost; advanced-node/advanced-packaging remains a bottleneck risk.
- **模型/估值影响：** Bull revenue of CNY 22,000 mn remains scenario-only; capacity uncertainty is one reason bull probability is not raised above 30%.
- **置信度与下次验证：** low；Company capacity/order disclosures, capex, supplier capacity comments and customer capex cross-check.

**Q5：At PE TTM 182x, what 3-5 year profit and ROIC path is implied, and is the disclosed annual profit/EPS proxy reliable?**
- **为何关键：** Valuation is the largest expectation-gap source; if the share count or profit period is inconsistent, the target and safety price change.
- **当前结论：** The current price embeds meaningful earnings recovery and long-duration high growth. Using the model's 2026E base EPS of 1.3337 and a 140x base multiple gives 186.72/share. Scenario-weighted fair value after reducing bear tail is 200.91/share. At 246.33 the expected return is -18.4%. The residual upside is a high-growth scarcity premium not supported by verified evidence.
- **采用证据：** EV044: PE TTM 182.23 / implied valuation data；EV045: PE percentile 64.2 and PB percentile 77.7；EV010/EV014: profit basis for 2026E EPS；Canonical share count: 2,324.3381 mn shares; calculated from market cap / close
- **最强反证：** Peers such as Cambricon ~191x and Montage ~95x are also expensive; official H1 profit growth plus AI capex may sustain a high multiple.
- **模型/估值影响：** Probability-weighted fair value changed from 194.83 to 200.91/share; expected return is -18.4%; recommendation Underweight.
- **置信度与下次验证：** medium；Authoritative total share count, latest TTM parent profit, Q3 2026 earnings and peer de-rating.

## Forecast Takeaways

### 预测结论

**1. H1 2026 demand was real, but the H2 bridge is not fully visible. A full-year bear case of only CNY 16,000 mn would require H2 revenue of about CNY 6,900 mn after an implied H1 of about CNY 9,099 mn, a sharp sequential collapse that is not supported by H1 evidence.**
- 证据锚：2026H1 R&D spend CNY 2,652.38 mn / 29.15% gives implied H1 revenue of about CNY 9,099 mn; official H1 revenue growth guidance was +55.56% to +70.20%; FY2025 revenue was CNY 14,376.89 mn.
- 财务含义：This supports lowering the bear probability from 25% to 20% while keeping base 2026E revenue at CNY 18,198.17 mn.
- 置信度与风险：Medium confidence. Invalidated if directly reported H1 revenue is materially below the R&D-based proxy or if H2 order intake stalls.

**2. Margin and cash conversion are the gating risks, not the direction of demand. The base net margin of 17.0343% applied to 2026E revenue gives CNY 3,099.93 mn parent profit, but negative H1 OCF and CNY 7,518.22 mn inventory mean that profit has not yet been proven to convert into cash.**
- 证据锚：Q1 2026 net margin 17.0343%, Q1 gross margin 55.604% down 5.59pp; 2026H1 OCF -427.54 mn; inventory CNY 7,518.22 mn.
- 财务含义：No 2026E OCF/FCF is accepted; the base multiple is capped at 140x and the expected value remains below the current price.
- 置信度与风险：Medium confidence. Cash-quality concern could be mitigated if the interim cash-flow notes show inventory is pre-sold and receivables are normal.

**3. The stock is still not cheap as a standalone entry even after trimming the bear tail. Scenario-weighted fair value is CNY 200.91/share against a price of CNY 246.33, leaving an expected return of about -18.4%.**
- 证据锚：Bull/base/bear fair values are 315.16/186.72/65.03; accepted probabilities are 30%/50%/20%; current price 246.33.
- 财务含义：The valuation does not justify new Buy/Overweight; the provisional action is Underweight/trim exposure until evidence closes the gap.
- 置信度与风险：Medium confidence. A verified DCU4 order ramp or margin stabilization could reduce or reverse the expected-return gap.

## Company Disaggregation

Economic unit map: (1) High-end processors, CPU and DCU, is the only reported consolidated segment and is 100% of disclosed revenue. Its revenue equation is volume x ASP x mix, but product-level volume, ASP and mix are not disclosed. Q1 2026 consolidated gross margin is 55.604%, operating margin 24.0529%, net margin 17.0343%. Inventory is CNY 7,518.22 mn and H1 OCF is -427.54 mn. (2) A second analytical unit, server/supernode system integration and delivery, is a possible ecosystem extension but has no separate reported revenue or margin. It should be treated as scenario optionality rather than core valuation. Ranked by revenue weight and valuation sensitivity, the processor unit dominates; system integration is second but unquantified. Customer concentration, channel/geography split and CPU/DCU product split are missing and remain research tasks.

## Moat Evidence Verdict

The moat is not proven enough to support the current multiple. X86 compatibility and customer switching costs are logically plausible but lack disclosed ASP, retention, share or repeat-purchase evidence. High R&D intensity is verified at 29.15% of revenue, but gross margin still fell 5.59pp and operating cash flow is negative, so R&D-to-profit conversion is only partial. Domestic security/qualification advantages are asserted but no segment market share or industry win-rate data are available. Therefore the moat should be treated as partial/unproven, not as a basis for paying an aggressive scarcity multiple.
